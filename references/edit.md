# Edits, references, sheets

Still skeleton is `image.md`. Shot skeleton is `video.md`.

## Edit

```text
CHANGE: <the single visible change>
KEEP: identity, pose, crop, background, every other object
MATCH: original light, perspective, grain
```

One change. If it misses, rewrite CHANGE. Do not stack a second change.

Point changes (jacket, scar, logo) are made by the model, then masked back onto the untouched original. An identity base does not take a second full pass. Two full passes make the face symmetrical and plastic, and that dead texture shows up later as bad acting.

## Roles

| Role | May copy | Must not copy |
|---|---|---|
| IDENTITY | face, hair, proportions | wardrobe, room, pose |
| STYLE | medium, palette, grain | people, logos, layout |
| PRODUCT | silhouette, logo, materials | scene, face |
| LIGHT | direction, color, hardness | subject |
| LOCATION | space, texture | angle, grade, people |
| MOTION | move from a clip | identity |
| VARIETY | a lineup of distinct people | one face as a crowd |

One identity image is one person. A crowd needs a variety sheet.

Label images in attachment order.

## Sheets

Character: face close-up, front body, back body. Grey, flat light, pores, no retouch.
Creature: two close-ups plus a size reference if scale against a human matters.
Location: 3/4, one anchor, one light. A reverse angle is a new plate matched to that light, not a new sun.
