# Stills

Skeleton. Omit empty blocks. Dialect is in `models.md`. Edits are in `edit.md`.

```text
FINISH: <poster | packshot | keyframe | sheet | local edit>
SUBJECT: <who or what, age-blind, one action>
PLACE: <where, time, weather if visible>
CAMERA: <height, distance, lens feel in words>
LIGHT: <one source, direction, hardness, color>
STYLE: <one medium or era, one palette>
TEXT: "<exact string>" at <position>
KEEP: <what must not change>
REFS:
  Image 1 — IDENTITY. Face and body only.
  Image 2 — STYLE. Palette and rendering only.
  Image 3 — PRODUCT. Shape, logo, materials.
```

Age-blind means no age number. Use build, hair, and wardrobe.

GPT layout jobs may replace the prose with one JSON object: `type`, `style`, `layout` regions, quoted strings. Shape is in `examples.md`.

Sheet laws:

- Grey, flat, no baked film grain on the identity plate. The look belongs in the scene.
- One readable face. Front full-body can be headless so the wide does not steal the face from a tiny head.
- 3/4, not straight-on, for both faces and locations.
- Location sheet needs an anchor (lamp, column, sofa) and one shadow direction.
